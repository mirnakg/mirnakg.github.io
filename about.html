About

Through this work, I've developed a deep appreciation for microbes as tiny factories that can be harnessed to solve big challenges.

I have also spent significant time building a foundation in machine learning, an area I am genuinely fascinated by and believe can significantly enhance scientific discovery.

For fun, I enjoy cooking, hiking, swimming, and lately, woodworking.
